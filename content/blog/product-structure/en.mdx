Unitcore is a handful of personal products — budget, lists, tasks, notes, habits — that
all run through one assistant in Claude. Underneath them sits a single idea: a space is a
circle of people and everything they keep together. You can be in several.

Everything below follows from that one idea.

## After you sign up

There is no setup step to get through. The account, the first space and the modules inside
it arrive together.

<Steps>
  <Step icon="user-plus" number="01" title="You sign up">One account, one login.</Step>
  <Step icon="square-dashed-bottom-code" number="02" title="A personal space appears">Created by default. Only you inside.</Step>
  <Step icon="toggle-right" number="03" title="Modules are already on">Every module belongs to the space, not to you.</Step>
  <Step icon="mail-plus" number="04" title="You invite someone">A new space for that circle — invite once, they see all of it.</Step>
</Steps>

## One person, two spaces

A space is not a folder, and it is not a workspace you switch between for tidiness — it is
a circle of people. Anna is in two of them, with one account:

<StructureNote>
  The same modules on both sides, the same Anna — a different circle of people. That is the
  only thing a space changes, and it is what keeps personal and shared apart.
</StructureNote>

## Which space the assistant works in

You never tell the assistant which space to look in. It works inside the one you are
already in, so the same sentence lands somewhere different depending on where you say it.

<Assistant
  title="One assistant in Claude"
  description="You talk to one assistant. It works inside whichever space you are in."
>
  <AssistantExample context="in Family">“Add milk to the shopping list.” → Family lists.</AssistantExample>
  <AssistantExample context="in Personal">“How much did I spend this month?” → your budget only.</AssistantExample>
</Assistant>

## Why it is built this way

The circle of people is drawn once, and everything else leans on it:

<Reasons>
  <Reason icon="shield">One privacy boundary for every product</Reason>
  <Reason icon="user-check">Invite once — it works across every module</Reason>
  <Reason icon="plug-zap">A new module switches on by itself — nothing to configure</Reason>
  <Reason icon="focus">The assistant sees exactly one context</Reason>
  <Reason icon="split">Personal and shared never mix</Reason>
  <Reason icon="log-out">Leave a space and the access is gone entirely</Reason>
</Reasons>

Connecting all of this to Claude is the other half of the story, and it takes a couple of
